      NYC CFB Bar Crawler

      Every FBS alumni club's game-watch bar in the city, set against the rest of the schedule. Pick your teams, choose a starting point, and chart a Saturday.

      How the crawl is chosen

      Kickoffs within 45 minutes of each other share a window, and each window gets one bar: the nearest one to where you were last. Bars that show more than one of your games come first, and your own team's bar wins over the opponent's. Any stop can split at halftime, so you can watch the second half of the same game, or another one, somewhere else. Tap an alternative to swap bars.

      Where the bars come from

      Alumni club and university pages first, then the bars' own pages and local press. Every bar shows its sources and how sure we are. Clubs move and bars close, so check before you go.
