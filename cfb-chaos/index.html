About CFB Chaos

Anyone who knows me knows I'm a huge college football fan, and on Saturdays I'm pulling for Team Chaos: higher-ranked teams losing to lower-ranked (or unranked) ones. The Chaotic Times is my weekly paper on it. Each section leads with something worth arguing about on the couch, and its "How I figured this" note has the details.

Sections

The poll (why the Top 25 has so many losses), Upsets (how many, where, and which ones were for real), Circles of suck (every conference's loop), The champs (Indiana's slide), Trends (parity, hangovers, blown leads), the Kiffin bowl (LSU vs. Ole Miss), and this week's ranked games.

Data

ESPN's AP polls, scoreboards, play-by-play, win probability, betting lines, and FPI, 2001–2026 (2020 left out). "Scoreboard rank" is my own rating built only from this season's scores. Logos from ESPN. The original 2016 version of this page started as a Python scraper and a couple of D3 charts.
